We read these off the plates; we are not specialists in either script. Nothing here has been corrected toward what the grammar expects — the 1825 is reproduced as printed, errors included.

What is being asked. For each row: does our transcription match the plate, character for character and point for point? Rows marked QUERIED are ones we already believe the printed source is wrong — there the question is what the page actually says, not what it ought to say.

Why we are asking rather than guessing. The Greek below contains the cautionary case. Two words would not resolve in our scan, so we set them with standard accentuation and flagged them unverified. A cleaner scan later showed the book prints a grave on the ultima in both — our reconstruction from the grammar was simply wrong. The flag prevented a false claim; it did not prevent a false reading.
