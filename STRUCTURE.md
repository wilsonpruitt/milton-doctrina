## Extent — MEASURED, not estimated

- **Latin body text: printed pp. 7–536.** Opens `LIBER PRIMVS. CAP. I.` at printed p.7 (pdf 23).
  Ends `TOTIUS OPERIS FINIS` at printed p.536 (pdf 552), followed immediately by an unpaginated
  then re-paginated **INDEX** (~printed 537–544, pdf 553–560), then publisher's advertisements
  (pdf 562+). The scan runs to 572pp total scan images, engraved facsimile plates believed to be
  among the front matter — not yet located precisely; confirm in M1 before relying on them for
  §6.4 apparatus.
- **English body text: printed pp. 9–711.** Opens `BOOK I. CHAPTER I.` at printed p.9 (pdf 65).
  Ends `THE END.` at printed p.711 (pdf 767), followed immediately by Sumner's own
  **ADDENDA ET CORRIGENDA** (pdf 769+) — worth reading; it is Sumner correcting himself against
  Milton's other prose, and may sharpen a headnote or two.
- **Liber I / Book I:** Latin pp. 7–386 (33 chapters). English pp. 9–526 (33 chapters).
- **Liber II / Book II:** Latin pp. 387–536 (17 chapters). English pp. 527–711 (17 chapters).

## The chapter crosswalk

Generated by `tools/build-crosswalk.py` → `tools/crosswalk-output.txt` (Markdown tables, La/En

**Chapter titles below are read off each volume's OWN table of contents (not cross-translated)
and spot-checked against the opening plate of several chapters** (I.i, II.viii, II.xv). The
`_djvu.txt` OCR mangles a number of titles in both TOCs (`De Hesipiscentia` for `Resipiscentia`,
`De Iiuitione` for `Insitione`, "69J" for 691, etc.) — the pdftotext + plate-check pass in this
session bypassed that OCR layer entirely except where noted below.

### Load-bearing sizing facts

- **★ I.v *De Filio Dei* (the Son of God) is the largest chapter by far in BOTH languages** —
  53 Latin pages (pp. 57–110) and 72 English pages (pp. 81–153), independently confirmed from two
  separate OCR/TOC sources agreeing. ~14% of Book I. This is the anti-Trinitarian chapter and the
  reason chunk ≠ chapter (see PLAN.md §5.1) and the reason it is scheduled LAST in M3.
- **The next-largest are I.iv *De Praedestinatione* (26/37pp) and I.x *De Gubernatione Speciali
  Hominis ante lapsum* (20/34pp, the Sabbath-and-marriage chapter) and II.iv *De Cultu Externo*
  (16/22pp).** Everything else in Book II runs 3–19pp, mostly under 12.
- **II.vi *De Zelo* is genuinely just 2 English printed pages (598–600)** — verified directly on
  the plate, not an OCR artifact. Its Latin counterpart runs 6 pages (440–446); the English
  compresses it. Note for M1: expect chapter-length ratios between the two languages to vary
  chapter to chapter, not hold a constant expansion factor.

### Two remaining OCR-uncertain page numbers — verify on the plate before use as anchors

Both are in the English Book II contents table; both are currently corrected by inference from
neighboring chapters and DO check out against the plate (see `raw/crosswalk-output.txt`), but
were not independently re-derived from a second source the way I.v was:
- **II.x** contents table shows a mangled `6*36` → read as **636**. Plate-verified indirectly
  (ch. IX opens 621, ch. XI opens 639, 636 is the only figure that fits).
- **II.xvi** contents table shows a mangled `69J` → read as **691**. Same indirect fit (ch. XV
  opens 680, ch. XVII opens 696).

Neither blocks M1; both should get a direct plate glance the first time their chunk is built.

## What M0 deliberately did NOT do

- Did not locate or verify the manuscript facsimile plates (§6.4 apparatus source) — flagged
  above, first M1/M3 session near the front matter should confirm their location and page range.
- Did not verify paragraph-level alignment between languages anywhere — only chapter boundaries.
  Per PLAN.md §10 risk 1, that verification happens per-chapter in M1/M3, not here.
- Did not source Wolleb's *Compendium* or Ames's *Medulla* (§6.2) — scoped as an M4 sub-task in
  the plan. Worth noting: Sumner's own front matter (English PDF, Preliminary Observations, ~p.
  xx of the roman-numeral front matter) independently states Milton's pupils copied "from a
  system of divinity which he collected from the most eminent writers upon that subject, as
  Amesius, Wollebius, &c." — external period confirmation of exactly the loci pairing the plan
  proposes, found incidentally during this session.
